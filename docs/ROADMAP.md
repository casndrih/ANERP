# ANERP roadmap

1. Foundation: Laravel deployment, PNG defaults, dashboard and core module schema.
2. Security and tenancy: authentication, company/branch context, granular permissions, audit trail, MFA and tenant-isolation tests.
3. Operations: product catalogue, warehouses, stock ledger and balances, supplier directory, purchase orders, maker-checker approval, partial/full goods receiving and inter-warehouse transfers.
4. HR and payroll: employee directory, leave applications, attendance, base-pay draft runs, maker-checker approval and payslip views. PNG PAYE and core superannuation rules are implemented from the Income Tax Act 2025 and BPNG notice; special cases and production compliance review remain.
5. Finance: company chart of accounts, balanced double-entry journals, supplier bills, customer invoices, bank accounts, bank transactions and statement reconciliation. Supplier payment allocation and maker-checker AP settlement are implemented. Fixed assets, supplier advances, GST return completeness and period close remain future work.
6. Reporting and integrations: Phase 6 baseline includes trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, bank cash movement, AP/AR ageing, approved payroll summaries, inventory movement reports and CSV export. XLSX/PDF, classified cash-flow statements, REST API, webhooks, POS, biometrics and banking adapters remain future work.
7. Production readiness: baseline role/permission matrix and activity audit logging implemented across core modules; PostgreSQL audit-event update/delete protection added; TOTP MFA and privileged-role enforcement implemented; automated secret/dependency/container checks and backup/restore runbook added. Monitoring, incident response, restore drills, disaster recovery, load testing, independent security review, accounting/payroll compliance review and user acceptance testing remain.
8. Advanced inventory controls: FIFO and moving weighted-average costing, company valuation policy, per-warehouse/product cost balances, FIFO receipt layers and exact movement valuation are implemented. Stock-count sessions and independent adjustment approvals are implemented.
9. Procurement and supplier invoice controls: three-way matching of approved PO prices, received quantities and supplier invoice lines; GRNI-to-AP posting; landed-cost allocation between remaining inventory and COGS. Domestic GST posting, eligible GST on PO-matched goods/landed-cost invoices, customs import assessments using actual assessed duty and import GST, a net-of-GST G1-style management summary, multi-line PO authoring, quantity/value/equal/weight/volume landed-cost allocation, supplier payments and supplier inventory credit notes/stock returns are implemented. Full G1 filing automation, section 65A withholding, private-use and exempt-sale input-credit apportionment, GST adjustment notes, OCR, automated HS/tariff classification and charge-specific landed-cost allocation remain future work.

Implementation branches are proposals until CI, migration and acceptance checks pass and the pull requests are reviewed and merged.
